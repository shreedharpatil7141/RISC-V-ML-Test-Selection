# Stage 13 active learning

Run `scripts/machine_learning/run_stage13_matlab.bat`, then the standalone `validate_stage13_matlab.bat`. The pipeline replays saved Stage 10 XSim and Verilator observations offline, with acquired-only refits and a frozen grouped 196/46/40 split. Simulator results remain separate. The 16-test initial subset and validation-selected settings are deterministic. Heldout test observations are revealed only after the complete policy ranking is frozen.

The frozen uncertainty policy acquired 8 tests per simulator for the one-shot heldout replay. It added zero reachable bins beyond initial coverage (167 of 277 covered), including zero new rare bins (47 of 153 were already covered). Observed cycles were 2,455 against a 2,193.6 predicted budget, a 261.4-cycle overrun. Training uncertainty replay increased coverage from 148 to 167 bins after eight acquisitions; random replay averaged 155 (standard deviation 8.38 across ten seeds). These offline observations are exploratory on a small corpus and do not show effective heldout prioritization, savings, superiority, or generalization. Stage 12 began at saturated coverage and its predicted cost budgets also exceeded observed execution costs; predicted budgets are estimates, not hard limits.
