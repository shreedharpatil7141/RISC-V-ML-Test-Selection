# Research Roadmap

Research question: can a pre-execution-feature ML model reduce the cost (tests/instructions) to reach target functional coverage relative to random and historical-greedy selection, and how close does it get to the oracle bound?

Planned analyses (Stage 14): K-means clustering of tests, cost regression, feature importance, rare-bin prediction, ablations (feature groups, model type, calibration), generalization to held-out profiles.
Threats to validity: processor-specific bins, synthetic generator bias, proxy cost vs wall-clock, replay vs live execution, small number of rare bins.
Paper outline (Stage 15): Introduction, Background, Framework, DUT and verification, Dataset, ML method, Experiments, Results, Limitations, Conclusion.
